🧠 Exploring Mental Health Data
This repository focuses on analyzing and visualizing mental health data to gain insights into trends, risk factors,
And the impact of mental health on different demographics. The goal is to use data-driven approaches to better understand mental 
health conditions, their prevalence, and associated socio-economic and workplace factors.

📌 Features
📊 Mental Health Trends & Statistics
Prevalence of Mental Health Disorders: Anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and more.
Demographic Breakdown: Mental health trends by age, gender, location, and socio-economic background.
Workplace Mental Health: Analyzing employer support, mental health leave policies, and workplace stressors.
Access to Mental Healthcare: Evaluating availability of therapy, insurance coverage, and barriers to treatment.

📍 Factors Affecting Mental Health
Economic & Financial Stress: Impact of unemployment, income level, and cost of living.
Social & Lifestyle Factors: Correlation between mental health and social isolation, relationships, and physical activity.
Substance Use & Addiction: Examining links between alcohol/drug use and mental health conditions.
Pandemic & Crisis Impact: Analyzing how COVID-19 and other global events have affected mental health.
